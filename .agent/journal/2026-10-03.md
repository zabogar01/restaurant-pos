## ARCH-005's answer and FE-036

The architect confirmed four of the eight draft rules (V1, V3, V6, V8) and corrected the rest.
The corrections are the substance of FE-036:

- **`reachedClosed` is narrowed and a second predicate added.** Left as `status !== 'open'`, the
  name would be false for a voided order. Worse, five readers of `isClosed` (`tableSlot`,
  `update`, the `close` pre-check, `changeDiscount`'s fact, and through `reachedClosed` the Back
  redirect) would treat a voided order as open: the table would never free, and a close or a
  discount change on it would report success.
- **No cancellation ticket in the book.** Nothing reads it, a `queued` delivery that can never
  advance is a claim, and the client already keeps no receipt on the same reasoning. The
  operation returns `cancels` (the FIRED line ids), and the B-16 rule is tested there.
- **Refusal order:** `unknown-line` and `not-fired` before `voidRule`, which throws on a voided
  line. An empty order, an all-voided order and a quick sale are not refusals; FR-H7 is the
  server's only.
- **A sheet with no subject is not drawn**, decided where `voiding` is computed so the frame is
  never inert under nothing. The `?state=approval*` addresses stay routing: they picture the
  prompt and carry a reason nobody chose.
- **The order void lands on the floor by replacing the history entry**, as Close does, and the
  PosRoutes redirect stays as the guard. Both void commands carry an idempotency key on the
  server (ADR-003), unlike the discount.
