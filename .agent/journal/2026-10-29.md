The lead's rulings (the task file's *The shape*, 1 to 10), each for the owner to overturn:
1. **No `display_order` and no reorder.** C1 sketched the column, but FR-B5 and ADR-012 rule 10 name no reorder. The

2. **The name is 1 to 32 code points**, unique among active presets, case-insensitively. 32 is the length of every

3. **An edit sends the whole preset** (name and value) with the version. An inactive preset may be edited.
4. **The wire value** is a discriminated union: `{ kind: 'PERCENTAGE', ratePpm }` or `{ kind: 'FIXED', amount }`. A
   kind–value mismatch is refused by the schema's strictness.
5. **The presets read is its own route** (`GET /api/back-office/config/catalog/presets`), not a new key in the strict
   `CatalogResponse` that BO-03 decodes.
6. **The domain lives in a new `discount-preset.ts`**, through `catalogWrite`. `catalog.ts` gains only `'PRESET'` and
   its index in `DUPLICATE_OF`.
