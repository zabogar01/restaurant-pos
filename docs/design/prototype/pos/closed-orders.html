Purpose

Find a closed order in order to reprint its receipt or refund it.

Ruling I-1 — approved

This screen is not named by any requirement; it exists because FR-G7 reprints on demand and FR-H5 refunds a closed order, and neither says how the order is found.

Search is by table, time and amount. Not by receipt number — the numbering format is an open product question and a search built on an unsettled key would have to be redone.

Fixed by the PRD

  • Refunded orders are terminal and marked as such. FR-H6, B-10
  • Voids and refunds against a closed business day are blocked. FR-H7, B-9
  • A zero-total order is a real closed order that appears here. FR-G11

Not visible in a still

Scope and retention are undecided. Drawn scoped to the open business day, which is the smallest scope that satisfies reprint and refund.
