Back office — 1440 wide

Purpose

Close the business day and produce the immutable report.

Fixed by the PRD

  • Close is refused while any order is open, and lists them. No force-close, no "close anyway", no auto-void of stragglers. FR-I2, AC-15
  • The close command reads authoritative server state inside its own transaction, so owning the back office does not weaken the check. FR-I2
  • A closed day is immutable. Its report never changes; voids and refunds against it are blocked. B-9, FR-H7, I4
  • A second close of the same day is rejected; the report stays printable. FR-I4
  • Post-close corrections are PRD Open Question 3. No adjustment flow is drawn.
Designer's call — I-6

The open-order list does not link into the POS. Two separately bootstrapped clients with separate sessions cannot hand an actor context to each other (NFR-5, FR-A2c), so a link would be a lie. It is a re-read on demand instead, with "Check again" naming what it does.

Designer's call — I-11

A confirmation step belongs here and nowhere on the POS: the reader is seated, the action is irreversible (B-9), and there is time to read. On the POS the equivalent protection is a PIN, never a dialog.
