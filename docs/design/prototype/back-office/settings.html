Back office — 1440 wide

Purpose

Currency, precision, tax and service-charge rates, payment methods, and the business details printed on receipts.

Ruling I-9 — payment methods

Custom named payment methods are configuration here, not free text typed on the fastest, most error-prone screen in the product. FR-G1, B-24

Fixed by the PRD

  • Currency and decimal places become immutable after the first order. The locked state is not an edge case; it is the state this screen spends its life in. FR-B1, M1
  • A rate change is versioned and never retroactive. No "apply to open orders" option. FR-B1, B-8, B-9
  • One tax rate, tax-inclusive, service charge untaxed. No tax groups, no per-item rates, no jurisdiction packs. PRD §4, B-3, B-4
  • Rate precision is one part per million. The permitted range is PRD Open Question 4 and is not the designer's to settle — drawn without a stated maximum.
  • Only cash may exceed the balance. FR-G3, G4, B-5

Not visible in a still

An order captures the settings version in force when it opened and keeps it for life. FR-B1, C7
