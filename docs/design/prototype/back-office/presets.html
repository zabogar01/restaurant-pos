Back office — 1440 wide

Purpose

Define the named discounts staff may apply without approval.

Fixed by the PRD

  • A preset is the constrained common path: any signed-in staff member applies it with no prompt. Presets are not approval-gated, here or at the POS. FR-F2, principle 3
  • Discounts are 0–100%; a fixed discount is capped at the subtotal; no order reaches a negative total. FR-M5
  • No scheduling, no auto-apply, no date or time triggers. A "valid from / valid to" field crosses the promotions boundary. FR-F7, B-21
  • One discount per order — no stacking rules, no precedence editor. B-22
  • Applying snapshots name, kind and value onto the order; later edits here never change past orders. FR-F4, F5, B-8, AC-17

Not visible in a still

Preset changes advance the catalog version, so a POS command built on an old preset value is rejected rather than applied at the wrong figure. FR-C7
