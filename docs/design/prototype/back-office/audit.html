Purpose

Answer "why is the till short?" the next morning.

Fixed by the PRD

  • Append only. No edit, no delete, no soft-delete, no hide. A designer must not draw a row action menu here. FR-J1, B-7
  • Audited actions are exactly: whole-order void, fired-line void, discount apply/replace/remove, refund, manager takeover of a checkout lease, and every manager-approval outcome. FR-J3
  • Removing a pending line is not audited and must not appear. FR-H2
  • A successful approved action is one combined entry, not two. A failed or cancelled approval is one entry with no approver. FR-J3, AC-18
  • Every entry names a specific person. No "system" actor, no action attributed to a terminal. B-13
  • No PIN value appears here in any form, including partially masked. FR-J4, B-12
  • Unauthenticated PIN failures and throttle cooldowns are security telemetry, not audit entries, and do not belong on this screen. FR-J3
Ruling I-3 — deferred, not forgotten

BO-14, a viewer for that security telemetry, is out of scope for the MVP. The store exists and is written to; nothing reads it this release. Recorded here so the omission is visible rather than looking like an oversight.
