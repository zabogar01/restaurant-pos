Back office — 1440 wide

Purpose

Define one item: category, tax-inclusive base price, variants, modifiers.

Fixed by the PRD

  • Prices are tax-inclusive. The editor asks for the price the customer pays. There is no ex-tax field. PRD §4, B-4
  • Variants are single-select with a positive or negative delta; modifiers are multi-select with a delta of zero or more. Two different structures, not one "options" concept. FR-C2, C3
  • Resolved unit price floors at zero. A negative variant delta snapshots zero, never a negative. FR-C2, AC-26
  • No nested modifier groups. FR-C4
  • No link between modifiers and stock. No inventory field. FR-C4
  • Currency and minor-unit precision are fixed elsewhere and locked after the first order. FR-B1, M1

Not visible in a still

  • Saving advances the catalog version, which can reject a POS command built on the old prices with CATALOG_CHANGED. FR-C7
  • Unsaved form state survives an idle re-authentication. FR-A2b
