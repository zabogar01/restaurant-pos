Back office — 1440 wide

Purpose

A landing destination after sign-in: the open business day, anything outstanding, and the route to end of day.

Designer's call — I-2

Not named by any requirement. Drawn because FR-I2 makes "which orders are still open?" the manager's most frequent question and FR-E3 requires a kitchen incident to be unmissable here. If it is cut, sign-in lands on the menu and the day state lives on BO-09.

Fixed by the PRD

  • The back office never operates orders. It observes them. No settle, void, fire, or refund control appears here. FR-G12
  • Kitchen emergencies appear in both clients, at emergency weight; receipt failures at lower urgency, never the same. FR-E3, E6, AC-33
  • An order belongs to the business day that was open when it was created. FR-I1
FR-A2b

Idle expiry is a modal over the work with unsaved form state preserved, not a bounce to the login screen. Background polling does not count as activity.
