Back office — 1440 wide

Purpose

Read one immutable end-of-day snapshot; print it.

Fixed by the PRD — the whole figure set

FR-I5 fixes every figure on this screen. None may be dropped:

  • Gross sales, including orders later refunded.
  • Refunds as a separate total; net = gross − refunds.
  • Gross, reversal and net for included tax, service charge and discounts.
  • Gross, refund allocation and net movement by payment method.
  • Order count for everything that reached closed, with refunded count separate.
  • Whole-order void count and value, at the total immediately before the void.
  • Fired-line void count, the recorded line value, and the before/after reduction in order total — these differ under a percentage discount or a service charge, and both must appear.

Gross / reversal / net is the structural spine of this screen. A designer must not collapse it into single figures.
